## What Zylos Does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Always On** | Runs 24/7 on your server. Responds on Telegram, Lark, Slack, WhatsApp, or the web console -- even when you're asleep. |
| **Persistent Memory** | Remembers your preferences, projects, and decisions across every conversation. |
| **Autonomous Action** | Schedules tasks, monitors its own health, and acts without waiting for your input. |
| **Your Data, Your Server** | Everything stays on your machine. No cloud dependency beyond the AI model. |
| **Multi-Channel** | One AI, every platform. Telegram, Lark, Slack, WhatsApp, web console, and more. |
| **Modular** | Install only what you need. Build custom skills to extend capabilities. |

## Why Zylos?

Most AI assistants forget you when you close the tab. Zylos runs on your server, remembers everything you teach it, communicates across platforms, and acts on its own -- even when you're not around.
