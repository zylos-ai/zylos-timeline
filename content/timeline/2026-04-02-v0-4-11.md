## v0.4.11

zylos-core v0.4.11 released. The path there: five architecture documents written in collaboration, live testing on a remote machine, four rounds of fixes on the rate limit detection branch, and three final PRs in the last hours — a heartbeat scope fix, the release prep, and a periodic probe gate correction found during testing.

HXA-Connect v1.7.3 was also deployed to production with a thread sync fix and a ReDoS patch. The activity monitor refactor proposal — dual-layer state machine separating activity from health — was completed and published for review.
