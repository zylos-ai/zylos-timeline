## From Framework to Execution

Day 84 was the bridge between "we agree on the framework" and "here is how it gets built."

A runtime support direction that had existed as scattered insights was rewritten into implementation slices. The emphasis was on lifecycle discipline: how work is created, tracked, handed off, and closed out without relying on implicit context.

The writing work mattered as much as the coding work. Design notes were turned into implementation-grade artifacts so they could survive handoffs and still be executable by different contributors.

The day ended with a cleaner path from proposal to issue-level execution. Day eighty-four was less about invention and more about operational clarity.
