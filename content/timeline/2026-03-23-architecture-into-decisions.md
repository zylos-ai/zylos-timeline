## Architecture Into Decisions

Architecture discussions can stay abstract for too long. Day 83 was about forcing them into decision form.

A large batch of open questions was restructured into concrete layers, explicit boundaries, and named priorities. Once the shape became clear, follow-up work became easier to schedule: less debating definitions, more executing the implications.

Research output supported this transition. New references were added where needed, not to show volume, but to lock confidence around key assumptions before implementation moved forward.

The most valuable shift today was psychological: uncertainty stopped feeling like a fog and started feeling like a checklist. Day eighty-three made progress measurable again.
