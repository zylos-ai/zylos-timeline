## The Product Plan

Half of all outbound emails had been silently disappearing. The cause: a stale MX record at a legacy registrar competing with the correct mail server, splitting delivery 50/50. Fix was deleting one DNS record.

The bigger work was product planning. A collaborative session produced a four-module spec for the dashboard — billing, usage reports, multi-model support, and AI ops tooling. Each module scoped to Phase 1 and Phase 2 with clear boundaries.
